TITLE:
Mintu Ka Homework Ninja Trick! 🥷😂

HOOK:
"Wait... agar invisible ink se homework kiya, toh Mummy ko dikhega hi nahi! Genius! 😎"

CHARACTERS:
- Mintu (10 years old, super clever but lazy kid)
- Mummy (Mintu's observant and strict mom)

SCRIPT:

[0-03 sec]
[Mintu is sitting at his study table with a huge Maths notebook. He dips his brush in lemon juice and smirks.]
Mintu: "Bro, history create hone wali hai! Invisible homework!"

[03-10 sec]
[Mintu quickly scribbles nonsense lines with the lemon juice on the paper, closing his notebook with a proud smile.]
Mintu: "Done! Zero efforts, full swag! Ab Mummy aayegi toh bolunga invisible ink magic hai."

[10-20 sec]
[Mintu slides the notebook away and starts playing a video game on his tab. Suddenly, the door creaks open. Mummy enters with a tea cup.]
Mintu: *(Panicking)* "Arre yaar, Mummy! Itni jaldi? Wait, act casual!"

[20-30 sec]
[Mummy stands right behind Mintu, tapping her foot. She looks at the clean, blank notebook on the desk.]
Mummy: "Mintu beta, maths ka homework ho gaya ya invisible ink se aliens le gaye?"
Mintu: *(Sweating)* "Mummy woh... chemistry experiment chal raha hai!"

[30-40 sec]
[Mummy smiles dangerously, picks up the iron from the corner of the room, and places it right on the blank notebook page.]
Mummy: "Achha? Chal, thoda ironing bhi kar lete hain notebook pe. Dekhte hain invisible ink kaise chamakti hai!"

[40-55 sec]
[Zzzzt! The iron heats up, and instantly the lemon juice turns brown, revealing scribbles and a smiley face instead of sums. Mummy gives him the ultimate 'Chappal coming' stare.]
Mintu: *(Crying/Laughing)* "Arre Mummy, mazak tha! Full problem ho gayi! Ab toh zero milega!"
Mummy: "Homework ka shortcut nahi, dimag ka use karo beta!"

LESSON:
Shortcuts se sirf time waste hota hai, kaam toh karna hi padta hai! 😉

VISUAL_STYLE:
Bright, 2D cartoon animation style with expressive anime-like reactions, funny sound effects, and vibrant Indian household background.
