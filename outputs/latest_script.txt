TITLE:
Mintu Ka Homework Hack! 🧠❌

HOOK:
Bro, Mintu ne aisi ninja technique lagayi ki homework khud-b-khud gayab ho jaye! But wait... mom ka radar on hai! 🚨

CHARACTERS:
- Mintu (10 years old, super clever, always finding shortcuts)
- Mummy (Ninja level mom with superpower hearing)

SCRIPT:

[0-03 sec]
[Mintu is looking at a heavy math notebook with a horror face. Zoom in on his expressions.]
Mintu: "Arre yaar! Ye math ka homework dekh ke na mujhe chakkar aa raha hai. Full torture hai ye!"

[03-10 sec]
[Mintu puts his physics book on his head, closing his eyes tightly.]
Mintu: "Idea! Agar main is book pe dhyan se so jaunga, toh saare formulas mere dimaag mein Bluetooth se transfer ho jayenge!"

[10-20 sec]
[Mintu's pet dog sniffs the math book and sneezes on it. Mintu opens one eye, smiling.]
Mintu: "Dekha Tommy? Doggy ne bhi bol diya—Math is dangerous! Ab toh pakka exemption milni chahiye."

[20-30 sec]
[Mintu hides under the blanket with a flashlight, playing a game on his phone instead of studying.]
Mintu: "Hehe, Mummy ko kya pata! Main toh undercover agent banke mission complete kar raha hoon."

[30-40 sec]
[Suddenly, the blanket is pulled away with lightning speed. Mummy is standing with hands on her hips, holding a wooden spoon.]
Mintu: "Wait... ye kya twist ho gaya?! Mummy, aap yahan teleport kaise ho gayi?!"

[40-55 sec]
[Mintu is sitting at the study table, crying dramatically while writing with both hands.]
Mintu: "Guys, kabhi shortcuts mat try karna! Warna Mummy ki chappal-guided missile se koi nahi bacha sakta!"

LESSON:
Shortcut ka chakkar, padega bhaari! Homework time pe khatam karo, warna Mummy ki entry fix hai! 😉

VISUAL_STYLE:
Bright, vibrant 2D cartoon animation style with exaggerated funny expressions, dramatic sound effects (like cartoon zoom and slip sounds), and fast-paced editing.
