TITLE:
Mintu Ka Homework Hack! 📝😂

HOOK:
Arre yaar! Ye homework kab khatam hoga? Mintu ne lagaya ek aisa jugaad, ki khud hi phas gaya! 

CHARACTERS:
- Mintu (9 saal ka ek super clever aur lazy bachha)
- Mummy (Super-mom, jinki baz jaisi nazar har jagah hai)

SCRIPT:

[0-03 sec]
[Visual: Mintu study table pe baitha hai, book khuli hai, aur wo yawning kar raha hai]
Mintu: (Camera ki taraf dekh kar) Bro, ye maths ka homework na, world war se bhi zyada tough hai! Kuch toh karna padega.

[03-10 sec]
[Visual: Mintu room mein idhar-udhar dekhta hai, phir usko ek brilliant idea aata hai]
Mintu: Idea! Agar main khud so jau, toh homework kaise hoga? Wait... ek pro hacker wala jugaad hai!

[10-20 sec]
[Visual: Mintu pillow ko apni hoodie pehnaata hai, blanket odha deta hai taaki lage wo so raha hai, aur khud chupke se bed ke neeche ghus jata hai]
Mintu: (Whispering, grinning) Hehe! Mummy ko lagega Mintu padh ke so gaya. Mission Homework Escape: SUCCESS!

[20-30 sec]
[Visual: Mummy haath mein paani ka glass lekar room mein aati hain. Wo bed pe leti 'dummy' ko dekhti hain]
Mummy: Mintu beta? Itni jaldi so gaya? Chal, thoda paani pee le... 
[Visual: Mummy pillow ko hilaati hain]

[30-40 sec]
[Visual: Blanket girta hai, sirf pillow aur hoodie nikalti hai! Mummy ka face shock se anger mein change hota hai]
Mummy: (Strict tone mein) Achha ji! Mintu sahab toh gayab hain! 
[Visual: Mummy turant jhuk kar bed ke neeche dekhti hain, jahan Mintu pakda jata hai]
Mintu: (Bed ke neeche se darr kar) Oops... Full panic mode on! 💀

[40-55 sec]
[Visual: Mummy Mintu ka ear-hold karke bahar khinchti hain, background mein funny sound effect]
Mummy: Beta, jugaad badhiya tha, par dimaag galat jagah lagaya! Ab double homework karega!
Mintu: (Rota hua expression dekar, audience ko bolta hai) Arre yaar! Koi mujhe bachao! 😭

LESSON:
Shortcut se kabhi kaam nahi banta, homework pehle, masti baad mein! 😉
